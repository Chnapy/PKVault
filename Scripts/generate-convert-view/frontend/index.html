PKVault Convert View

Big view showing PKVault convert results, with all species (1000+) and forms, covering a maximum of use cases.
Every PKM generated is displayed, with all its properties.
Changed properties from previous PKM are highlighted to identify possible unexpected changes.
Multiple convert suites are done. 4 types of suite exist:

  • FORWARD: From a generation to a new one (like G2->G8)
  • BACKWARD: From a generation to an old one (like G9->G1)
  • BASE_TO_VARIANT: From a primary game to a secondary one (like G7->G7b, aka Sun->Let'sGo)
  • VARIANT_TO_BASE: From a secondary game to a primary one (like G7b->G7, aka Let'sGo->Sun)
